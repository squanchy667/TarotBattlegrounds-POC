## 3.0 Voice and Tone
**Voice:** Precise and architectural. We do not "cast" spells; we "align" them.  
**Tone:** The quiet confidence of a master engineer.

### Copy Principles
- Use mechanical verbs: calibrate, insert, align, rotate, lock, forge.
- Macro-scale: talk about the cosmos, orbits, and constellations, not just the table.
- Technical magic: treat magic as high-end power (plasma/starlight), not whimsical sparkles.

### Approved Lexicon
Mechanism, Orbit, Alignment, Blueprint, Horizon, Lens, Refraction, Sequence, Chrono-Lock, Constellation.

## 5.0 Visual Direction: "Celestial Clockwork"
**Concept:** A battle played inside an ancient, high-tech astronomical device.

**Materials:** Polished Brass, Oxidized Copper, Deep Blue Velvet, Sapphire Glass, Starlight.

### Visual Pillars
- The grid is radial: move away from square grids. Interfaces should feel circular, orbiting a central core.
- Magic is light: effects should look like lasers passing through lenses or liquid starlight, not dust or glitter.
- UI is physical: every button is a metal plate. Every window is a glass lens.

## 8.0 UI and Layout Patterns
### 8.1 The "Card" Design
Cards are not paper. They are brass cartridges.  
Border: Heavy brass with gear-tooth details.  
Center: A convex glass lens showing the character or spell.  
Animation: When played, they click and lock into the slot mechanically.

### 8.2 Buttons and Interactive Elements
Primary Button: A brass plate with inset shadow to feel heavy. Text is stamped.  
Secondary Button: A glass panel with a thin copper rim.  
Dividers: Avoid straight lines. Use the "Gear-Work Divider" (interlocking gears and starlight rails).

### 8.3 Layout Geometry
- Use circular frames for avatars.
- Use radial menus where possible.
- Backgrounds should feature subtle, rotating blueprint lines.

## 11.0 Visual Do's and Don'ts
**DO**
- Use glowing blue plasma for magic effects.
- Make UI elements feel heavy and physical.
- Use astronomical and astrological motifs (constellations, orbits).
- Keep lighting warm (brass) vs cold (void) for contrast.

**DON'T**
- Use parchment paper or scrolls.
- Use generic high-fantasy fonts (Papyrus-style).
- Use rust, dirt, or grime textures. This machine is well-maintained.
- Use neon green or hot pink.
